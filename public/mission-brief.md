# Mahabharata — Day 14
Draft 0.1 · Collaborator's first demonstration mission

## The ambition
Create a community-built Mahabharata game with scale, depth, and moral complexity. Begin with one small playable moment of Day 14 on Kurukshetra.

## First milestone
Agree on a source-grounded creative brief and explore one archery mechanic. A full AAA game remains a long-term ambition. There is no playable build yet.

## Shared direction
- Respect the source and distinguish textual evidence from interpretation.
- Express Arjuna's mastery through timing, precision, and control.
- Treat Astras as rare and frightening.
- Prefer a small, testable moment over a sprawling incomplete world.
- Keep community sharing and noncommercial intent visible. Final contribution and licensing terms are not yet established.

## Starting contributions
1. Lore: provide a source reference, interpretation, and one playable expression of Gandiva's qualities.
2. Creative direction: prepare a concise mood brief for Day 14 as sunset approaches. Label modern adaptations and artistic invention.
3. Prototyping: sketch or build one arrow-volley experiment. Include controls, tools, and reproduction instructions.

## Resource rules
Experiments use contributors' own resources. No pooled budgets, hosted agents, or automatic compute spending are enabled in Increment 0.

## Review
Future shared submissions will be proposed changes. Implementation maintainers accept changes into a shared revision; community preferences inform review. Forks preserve attribution and applicable contribution terms.
